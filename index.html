Frequently Asked Questions

1. Kya solar panels par Govt Subsidy milti hai? +

Haan, PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana ke tahat residential gharon par sarkar dwara bhari subsidy di ja rahi hai, jisse aapki investment cost bohot kam ho jaati hai.

2. Solar panels ki life kitni hoti hai? +

Uchh quality wale Solar panels ki performance warranty 25 saal tak hoti hai, aur ye uske baad bhi kaam karte rehte hain.

3. Kya baarish ya badal hone par solar kaam karta hai? +

Baarish ya badal me production thodi kam ho jaati hai par band nahi hoti. On-Grid system me aap bachi hui power grid se le sakte hain, isliye bijli kabhi band nahi hoti.
